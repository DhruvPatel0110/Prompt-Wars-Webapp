CASE STUDY 1: College Tech & Cultural Fest Participation Crisis
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Domain: Event Management & Social Media Marketing

Scenario:
A university is organizing its flagship 3-day annual festival in 30 days. Last year suffered from poor turnout (only 350 students). This year the team must attract at least 900 attendees on a ₹20,000 budget, despite two rival college fests happening on the exact same weekend.

Key Metrics & Constraints:
- Target: 900+ registered attendees
- Budget: ₹20,000 strictly capped
- Timeline: 30 days
- Team: 25 active student volunteers
- Constraint: 2 rival college fests on the exact same weekend

30-Second Sabotage ("The Bomb"):
🚨 BUDGET EMERGENCY: ₹20,000 SLASHED TO ₹4,000!
The college finance committee froze funds! Your marketing budget is slashed from ₹20,000 down to ₹4,000. All paid flyers, posters, and paid influencer promotions are immediately revoked.

30-Second Solve / Directive:
Pivot immediately to 100% zero-cost guerrilla tactics, inter-department meme wars, WhatsApp status chains, and "Squad Pass" group referral bounties.



CASE STUDY 2: Campus Electric Scooter Rental & Green Mobility Launch
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Domain: Clean-Tech & Operations Strategy

Scenario:
A student startup is launching a shared electric scooter rental service across a sprawling university campus and nearby hostels. Students currently rely on expensive autos or long walks. The startup must prove reliability and overcome student skepticism about battery range.

Key Metrics & Constraints:
- Fleet Size: 100 smart electric scooters
- Charging Hubs: 6 strategic campus hubs planned
- Target: 500 daily active riders in Month 1
- Budget: ₹35,000 marketing & ops reserve
- Timeline: 3 weeks to launch
- Constraint: Hostel curfews (9:30 PM) and battery range anxiety

30-Second Sabotage ("The Bomb"):
🚨 INFRASTRUCTURE HALT: 4 OUT OF 6 HUBS DENIED PERMITS!
Campus security blocked electrical connections for 4 out of your 6 charging stations due to power load concerns! You must launch with only 2 operational swap hubs.

30-Second Solve / Directive:
Adapt your operations by introducing mobile battery delivery vans, designated hostel drop zones, and ₹20 ride-credit incentives for riders who return scooters directly to the 2 active hubs.
